## Cinematic reel grades

The app's existing `*.jpg` backgrounds are color-graded from the Unsplash source
photos listed above. The three added photos are bundled at 3000 pixels wide as
`lake_dawn.jpg`, `haram_night.jpg`, and `misty_valley.jpg`. Each is preserved
alongside a subtle color and brightness adjustment as its `*_cinematic.jpg`
rendering variant; the preset gallery and renderer use that variant while the
original remains available as a high-resolution asset.

The silent loops in `../background-videos/` are locally generated motion
derivatives of these credited project assets: `misty_forest.jpg`,
`marble_arch.jpg`, and `sahara_moon.jpg`. They use the same Unsplash License
terms and retain the corresponding photo credits above. They were created from
local files; no additional stock media was downloaded.
